# Usability test (round 8, G)

5–8 players, 30 minutes each, screen-shared: 2 FACEIT level 5–10 · 2 Premier mid-rank · 2 Deadlock · 1–2 brand-new.

## Tasks (say them, don't show where)
1. Load one of your demos (or a match ID).
2. Find your biggest mistake in that match.
3. Explain one coach card back to me in your own words.
4. Solve 3 puzzles.
5. Find your K/D trend on your profile.

## Ask
- "What did you expect this button to do?" (after any hesitation)
- "Which mark felt wrong?"
- "What would make you come back tomorrow?"

## Write down
Per player: where they hesitated (screen + second), their words (quotes, exact), what they never found. Afterwards: the
top 5 problems, each with 1–2 quotes, into the next list. Never invent quotes or testimonials.

Later: a Discord survey (100+), a card sort (15+ players) for the nav names (Tactical / Meta / Recap).
